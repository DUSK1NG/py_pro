Schematic sketch (not to scale). Dashed curve shows exaggerated downward deflection for illustration only.

Assumptions

Linear elastic Euler–Bernoulli beam theory, small deflections, and a single downward concentrated load at midspan. Upward support reactions are positive. Downward deflection is negative in the source sign convention; this page reports the magnitude of the maximum downward deflection. This is an educational example.
